Escrow, payouts, payroll

Money that splits itself.

Every deal carries ordered split rules. When money lands — arras at the promesa, the balance at escritura, rent on the fifth — the ledger applies them and the accountant releases one batch.

Straight paymentOne participant, one amount. The photographer's COP 1.850.000 package, paid from the marketing budget at listing.
Shared commissionSeveral rules summing to 100 % of the agency's gross: listing broker, selling broker, agency, lawyer. Paid from escrow at close.
Referral feeA % or fixed amount to whoever referred, deducted before commission rules apply.
Staged splitsThe same person twice: 10 % at listing as a marketing advance, 30 % at close. Rental admin fee and salaries run monthly.
Client funds sit in a segregated escrow ledger. Releases need the accountant; anything over COP 50.000.000 also needs the owner.
